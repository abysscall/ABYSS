ETD-001 — Eligibility Declaration

3. Jurisdiction and Eligibility. I confirm I am not subject to a status for which participation is prohibited under applicable law or ABYSS's function-specific policy, and will not misrepresent my jurisdiction.

ABYSS Eligibility Policy

ABYSS determines feature access, not personal identity. A phone number is used only as one technical signal for a preliminary eligibility check — it is not proof of citizenship, residence, or tax residency.

Identifying information used for this check is not retained beyond what is necessary to complete it, unless a specific legal basis requires otherwise for a given function.

Restrictions apply to persons whose participation is prohibited by applicable law or by the rules of the specific ABYSS function being accessed — this can differ by function and by jurisdiction.
