# ABYSS — User Eligibility & Truthfulness Declaration

**Document ID:** ETD-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft — pending legal review before production use
**Related:** ADR-0025 (ABYSS Identity Gateway)

---

## Purpose

This declaration governs the user's own representations made during
ABYSS Eligibility Verification (ADR-0025) — a technical and legal
process distinct from KYC. It is a standalone document, separate from
AC Risk Warning (`docs/legal/AC-RISK-001.md`), Terms of Use, and Privacy
Policy, so each can be versioned and updated independently.

It does **not** attempt to exclude any ABYSS obligation that cannot
lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law (see Section 9).
Its purpose is narrower: it places responsibility for the truthfulness
of a user's own submitted information, and for any attempt to
circumvent eligibility restrictions, on that user.

This declaration is a self-certification mechanism. It is not a
substitute for the stronger verification required for the token
investor round, which is governed separately (see
`docs/PRESALE_STRATEGY.md`).

## Full Declaration Text

**1. Accuracy of Information**
I confirm that all information provided by me for ABYSS Eligibility
Verification is true, accurate and current.

**2. Minimum Age**
I confirm that I meet the minimum age required by the laws of my
jurisdiction to use the relevant ABYSS functions.

**3. Jurisdiction and Eligibility**
I confirm that I am not a resident of, or otherwise subject to, a
jurisdiction or status for which participation in the relevant ABYSS
function is prohibited under applicable law or ABYSS's function-specific
policy, and that I will not intentionally misrepresent my country of
residence, jurisdiction, or eligibility status.

**4. No Circumvention**
I will not use another person's credentials, identity information,
telephone number, VPN, proxy, or other technical means for the purpose
of circumventing ABYSS eligibility, jurisdictional, regulatory, or
security restrictions. This does not prohibit the use of VPNs or similar
tools for purposes unrelated to circumventing these restrictions.

**5. Not a Prohibited Person**
I confirm that I am not a person who is prohibited from using the
relevant services or participating in the relevant activity under
applicable law, including applicable sanctions or restricted-party
regimes.

**6. Personal Responsibility**
I acknowledge that I am solely responsible for the truthfulness,
accuracy, and legitimacy of the information and credentials I provide.

**7. Consequences of False Information**
I understand that ABYSS applies reasonable technical measures to
restrict access by persons not entitled to participate, and that ABYSS
may deny, suspend, or terminate my access where it reasonably determines
that eligibility information has been falsified, manipulated, or used
to circumvent applicable restrictions. Where I intentionally provide
false information or intentionally circumvent these restrictions, I
bear responsibility for that action to the extent permitted under
applicable law.

**8. No Authorization Through Misrepresentation**
I understand that access obtained through deliberate misrepresentation
or circumvention does not constitute valid authorization by ABYSS to
use restricted functions, regardless of whether the eligibility check
was technically passed.

**9. Applicable Law**
Nothing in this declaration limits or excludes any right, obligation,
or liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded under
applicable law, including obligations of ABYSS as an offeror under
Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCA) or equivalent applicable law.

**10. Changes in Eligibility**
I agree to provide updated information, or to stop using the relevant
function, if circumstances relevant to my eligibility status change.

## What Is Recorded — Verify → Declare → Forget

Per ADR-0025, identifying information used for the eligibility check
(e.g. a phone number) is **not retained** beyond what is necessary to
complete the check, unless a specific legal basis requires otherwise for
a specific function (open question — see ADR-0025).

What is retained against the pseudonymous `ABYSS_ID`:

```
DECLARATION_DOCUMENT   → "ETD-001"
DECLARATION_VERSION    → "1.0"
ACCEPTED_AT            → timestamp
ELIGIBILITY_RESULT     → ALLOWED / DENIED
```

If ETD-001 is later updated to version 1.1, a user's earlier acceptance
remains tied to the version they actually accepted — old records are not
retroactively upgraded.

**Data protection note.** Data and technical records are retained only
for as long as necessary to fulfil the stated purpose, comply with
applicable legal obligations, prevent abuse, and protect ABYSS's
legitimate interests. Where applicable law permits erasure or
restriction of processing, a request may be directed to the controller.
Whether the minimal event record above constitutes personal data under
GDPR in this specific architecture (e.g. if combinable with session/IP
logs) must be confirmed with counsel before this goes live — this is
not assumed either way by this document.
