# ABYSS — AC Risk Warning

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft — pending legal review before production use
**Related:** ETD-001 (Eligibility Declaration), `docs/PRESALE_STRATEGY.md`

## Purpose

This is a separate document from ETD-001. ETD-001 concerns the
truthfulness of the user's own submitted information. This document
concerns the user's understanding of the risks specific to AC as an
asset. Keeping them separate allows each to be versioned and updated
independently, and keeps the eligibility question distinct from the
investment-risk question.

## On-Screen Summary

> **AC Risk Disclosure**
> Before continuing, confirm the following.

**[ ] I understand that AC is an early-stage digital asset with no
guaranteed value, liquidity, or future price.**

`[ ACCEPT & CONTINUE ]`

## Full Text

**1. No Guarantee of Value**
I understand that ABYSS does not guarantee the price, value, liquidity,
or future availability of AC on any market or exchange.

**2. Early-Stage Project**
I understand that the ABYSS protocol is under active development, that
mainnet does not yet exist, and that features described in ABYSS
materials may change, be delayed, or not be completed.

**3. No Investment Advice**
I understand that nothing in ABYSS's materials constitutes investment,
financial, tax, or legal advice, and that I am solely responsible for
my own decision to acquire or use AC.

**4. Risk of Total Loss**
I understand that I may lose some or all value associated with AC, and
that I should not participate with funds I cannot afford to lose.

**5. Regulatory Uncertainty**
I understand that the regulatory treatment of AC may vary by
jurisdiction and may change over time, and that ABYSS's ability to offer
certain functions in certain jurisdictions may change as a result.
