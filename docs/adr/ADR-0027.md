# ADR-0027 — AC Economy Working Draft and Wallet Integration Boundary

**Status:** Proposed  
**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Related:** ADR-0011, ADR-0012, ADR-0024, ADR-0026, RFC-0001, RFC-0002

---

## Context

AC v0 currently models a 55M AC cap, a 30M team reserve, and a 25M public
sale. The AC Economy work is considering a different allocation and Wallet
utility design. At the same time, Core, Consensus, Wallet, ABYSS OS, and AI
runtimes must remain composable without creating lateral Protocol Layer
dependencies or giving an AI uncontrolled financial authority.

## Proposed decision

Record the 25M Sponsorship Rewards / 15M Team / 15M Ecosystem allocation, the
five sponsorship stages, and the current-balance-based team-release direction
as an explicitly **non-binding working draft**. The draft is represented by
`AcEconomyWorkingDraft` and documented in
`docs/tokenomics/AC_ECONOMY_WORKING_DRAFT.md`.

The working draft must coexist with AC v0. It does not modify
`TokenomicsPlan::abyss_default()`, public-sale data, existing investor flows,
genesis state, or `Coin::MAX`.

Wallet levels are an access/utility proposal, never a paid security tier. All
levels retain high baseline security. A future protected transfer is an
explicit pre-settlement action; it cannot cancel a final chain transaction.

All human and AI requests, including requests from Claude and Kimi, remain
untrusted intent. They must pass through the ABYSS OS Policy Engine and the
account holder's authorization policy before Wallet signing or protocol
execution. AI may not receive implicit authority from a token balance, Wallet
tier, risk score, or draft economic parameter.

## Compatibility constraints

1. Preserve ADR-0024 Protocol Layer isolation. No Tokenomics ↔ Wallet ↔
   Consensus sibling-crate dependency is introduced.
2. Core is the source of truth for settlement and finality; Consensus remains
   blind to Wallet and economics semantics.
3. Until a separate ADR creates a versioned shared interface, integration is
   performed only in the Application Layer with explicit draft/active status.
4. No contributor may turn an open parameter—particularly Year 7+ releases,
   tier measurement, grace period, or protected-transfer behavior—into a
   production default.
5. Acceptance requires a migration plan for AC v0 data, published test
   vectors, legal review of any contribution flow, and coordinated sign-off by
   Core, Wallet, OS/Policy, and AC Economy owners.

## Consequences

- Teams can review and test the new economic direction without breaking
  existing devnet behavior or public integrations.
- The exact 25M / 15M / 15M amounts are stored as AC values, avoiding a
  misleading basis-point rounding convention.
- Wallet and OS teams receive one clear boundary for tier and
  protected-transfer work rather than competing local implementations.
- ADR-0012 remains the accepted AC v0 decision until this ADR is accepted and
  explicitly supersedes or amends it.
